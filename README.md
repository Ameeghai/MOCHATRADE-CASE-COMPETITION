# MochaTrade LaunchPad: Pre-Launch Growth Strategy

**Turning the wait before launch into participation, community, and reward.**

## Overview

MochaTrade LaunchPad is a proposed pre-launch engagement strategy designed to address waitlist dormancy and keep prospective users engaged while a trading platform prepares for launch.

Instead of relying entirely on repeated email reminders, LaunchPad proposes an interactive experience built around learning, prediction polls, progress tracking, community participation, and rewards.

The strategy focuses on two objectives: re-engaging existing dormant waitlist users and preventing new sign-ups from losing interest before launch.

## Problem Statement

The period between joining a waitlist and a product launch can weaken user interest. Repeated reminders may lose effectiveness, while potential users have few opportunities to interact with the product or its community.

LaunchPad addresses this gap by giving users meaningful activities to participate in before the main platform launches.

## Proposed Solution

The concept includes the following components:

- **User reactivation:** Personalized outreach and low-friction entry points, including one-tap WhatsApp prediction polls.
- **Engagement and habit formation:** Daily polls, streaks, quizzes, and participation-based activities.
- **Progress tracking:** A readiness indicator and user progress dashboard.
- **Community building:** Social challenges, discussion spaces, and community activities.
- **Rewards and incentives:** Proposed credits, badges, and rewards to encourage continued participation.
- **Launch-day transition:** A proposed mechanism for transferring eligible progress and rewards into the main platform.

## User Journey

1. **Discover:** Reach existing waitlist users and onboard new sign-ups.
2. **Enter:** Direct users into the proposed LaunchPad experience.
3. **Participate:** Encourage learning, polls, challenges, and community engagement.
4. **Build momentum:** Use progress indicators and rewards to encourage repeat participation.
5. **Transition:** Carry eligible progress into the main platform at launch.

## Growth and Retention Principles

The strategy draws on several behavioural concepts:

- Personalization and relevance
- Habit formation through recurring activities
- Progress visibility and goal tracking
- Social belonging and community participation
- Incentives and recognition
- Reduced friction during onboarding

These are strategic design principles, not experimentally validated results from a live product.

## Key Strategic Considerations

A real implementation would require testing engagement frequency, reactivation rates, onboarding conversion, retention, reward costs, and the effectiveness of different communication channels.

User consent, messaging preferences, financial-promotion rules, and responsible engagement would also need to be considered.

## Project Scope
